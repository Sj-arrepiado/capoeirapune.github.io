What is Capoeira?

A living dialogue of movement & rhythm

Capoeira is more than a martial art — it's a living dialogue of movement, rhythm, and expression. Practiced in a circle called the roda, two players exchange kicks, defenses, and playful tricks while musicians play the berimbau and atabaque.

It builds agility, coordination, balance, and confidence, while cultivating empathy and respect. Whether you're a child discovering movement or an adult rediscovering joy, Capoeira welcomes everyone into its rhythm.

400+ Years of history
History

Born from resistance,
alive in rhythm

Capoeira was born in Brazil over 400 years ago, created by African slaves who disguised self-defense training as dance. Under watchful eyes, they turned resistance into rhythm, struggle into song, and survival into art.

Today it represents unity and heritage. Through songs, movements, and rituals passed across generations, it tells a story of courage — every roda a living echo of that spirit.

"In Capoeira, we don't compete — we complete each other through movement and music." — Philosophy of Grupo Capoeira Brasil

About Us

Founded in passion, built on community

Grupo Capoeira Brasil Pune is led by Instrutor Arrepiado under the guidance of Mestre Caxias, bringing Brazilian Capoeira to India. The global group was founded in 1989 in Rio de Janeiro and now spans 40+ countries.

Guiding Master
Mestre Caxias

One of the key figures of Grupo Capoeira Brasil, known for his charisma, deep knowledge, and lifelong dedication. With decades of experience he has taught thousands worldwide. He visits Pune regularly for workshops and the annual Batizado, bringing wisdom and connection to our global community.

Instructor · Pune
Instrutor Arrepiado

Practicing Capoeira for over 14 years and trained under Mestre Caxias, Arrepiado represents Grupo Capoeira Brasil in Pune. His teaching builds character, confidence, and community. He has organized numerous workshops, events, and annual Batizados — creating a growing family of capoeiristas who embody respect, creativity, and passion.
