🌀 What is Capoeira?

Capoeira is more than just a martial art — it's rhythm, movement, culture, and community all woven together.
It’s where music meets motion, discipline meets fun, and everyone finds a place to grow.

Instrutor Arrepiado (Sagar Jain)

14+ years of Capoeira, 12+ years teaching, representing Grupo Capoeira Brasil under Mestre Caxias.

Founder of Capoeira Brasil Pune, Sagar brings a blend of discipline, joy, and tradition to every class.

Why Capoeira? It builds fitness, agility, and confidence—while connecting you to a global community and rich culture.
