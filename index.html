What is Capoeira?
Capoeira is an art form combining elements of martial arts, dance, acrobatics, and music. Developed by enslaved Africans in Brazil, it’s played in a circle (roda), with two players exchanging movements to the rhythm of traditional instruments and songs. Capoeira is more than just a sport—it’s a celebration of freedom, community, and creativity.
History of Capoeira
Capoeira originated in the 16th century as a form of resistance and cultural expression. Enslaved Africans disguised their training as dance to avoid punishment. Over centuries, Capoeira grew, survived bans, and became a symbol of resilience and unity. Today, it’s recognized worldwide as an important part of Brazil’s heritage.
Capoeira in Pune
Capoeira was introduced to Pune in the early 2010s. With regular classes, workshops, and annual Batizado events, the community has grown to welcome students of all ages. Led by Instrutor Arrepiado under Grupo Capoeira Brasil, Capoeira Pune is known for its inclusive spirit and cultural celebration.

Lineage & Philosophy
Grupo Capoeira Brasil’s philosophy blends tradition and innovation. The lineage traces back to Mestre Bimba and other legendary figures. The school emphasizes respect, discipline, open-mindedness, and the joy of sharing Capoeira with the world.
Instruments of Capoeira
Capoeira uses three main instruments: Berimbau (musical bow), Atabaque (drum), and Pandeiro (tambourine). The berimbau leads the rhythm, signaling changes in the game. Students learn to play and respect each instrument as part of their Capoeira journey.
Songs & Lyrics
Songs are sung in Portuguese, sharing stories of Capoeira’s roots, heroes, and philosophy. Lyrics encourage unity, respect, and joy. Common songs include “Paranauê,” “Zum Zum Zum,” and “Berimbau Metal.”
Capoeira Rhythms
Capoeira rhythms (toques) set the energy and style of the roda. The berimbau plays different patterns: Angola (slow, playful), São Bento Grande (fast, athletic), and others. Learning to recognize and follow these rhythms is part of becoming a Capoeirista.
