Capoeira ▶
What is Capoeira?
Capoeira is an Afro-Brazilian art combining martial arts, dance, acrobatics, and music. Played in a circle (roda), two players exchange movements to the rhythm of traditional songs and instruments.
History of Capoeira
Capoeira originated in the 16th century as a creative form of resistance. Enslaved Africans disguised their training as dance. Today, Capoeira is a symbol of freedom, community, and culture worldwide.
Capoeira in Pune
Capoeira arrived in Pune in the early 2010s, growing into a vibrant community. Regular classes, workshops, and Batizado events welcome students of all ages under Grupo Capoeira Brasil.
Benefits of Capoeira
  • Fitness: strength, flexibility, coordination
  • Well-being: discipline, focus, stress relief
  • Creativity: improvisation, musicality, self-expression
  • Community: friendship, teamwork, culture
  • Accessible for all ages and abilities

Lineage & Philosophy
Grupo Capoeira Brasil blends tradition and innovation. The lineage traces back to Mestre Bimba and other legends. The school emphasizes respect, discipline, and joy.
Music ▶
Instruments of Capoeira
Capoeira uses Berimbau, Atabaque, and Pandeiro. The berimbau leads, signaling rhythm and changes in the game. Students learn to play and respect each instrument.
Songs & Lyrics
Songs are sung in Portuguese, sharing stories of Capoeira’s roots, values, and heroes. Lyrics encourage unity, respect, and joy. Common songs include “Paranauê” and “Zum Zum Zum.”
Capoeira Rhythms
Capoeira rhythms (toques) set the energy and style of the roda. Berimbau patterns include Angola (slow), São Bento Grande (fast), and more.
