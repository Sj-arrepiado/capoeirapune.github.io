Capoeira

Capoeira is a beautiful fusion of martial art, dance, music, and culture. Born in Brazil, it’s a rhythmic game of movement, self-expression, and connection.

What is Capoeira?

Capoeira is more than a martial art — it’s a living dialogue of movement, rhythm and expression. Practiced in a circle called the roda...

About

Grupo Capoeira Brasil Pune is a space where movement, culture, and community come together.

Music

Music is the heartbeat of Capoeira. The berimbau, atabaque, pandeiro, agogô and reco-reco set the rhythm and mood for the roda.
