Capoeira
Capoeira is the art of movement, music, and community. Discover its story, roots, philosophy, and vibrant tradition in Pune.
What is Capoeira?
Capoeira is an Afro-Brazilian art combining martial arts, dance, acrobatics, and music. Played in a circle (roda), two players exchange movements to the rhythm of traditional songs and instruments.
History
Capoeira originated as a creative form of resistance. Enslaved Africans disguised their training as dance. Today, Capoeira is a symbol of freedom and culture worldwide.
Capoeira in Pune
Capoeira arrived in Pune in the early 2010s, growing into a vibrant community. Regular classes, workshops, and Batizado events welcome students of all ages.
Philosophy
Capoeira is about respect, discipline, and joy. It connects people across backgrounds, teaching cultural appreciation and creative expression.

Music
The heartbeat of Capoeira—the berimbau, drums, and songs that bring the roda to life.
Instruments
Capoeira uses Berimbau, Atabaque, and Pandeiro. The berimbau leads, signaling rhythm and changes in the game. Students learn to play and respect each instrument.
Common Songs
Songs are sung in Portuguese, sharing stories of Capoeira’s roots and heroes. Lyrics encourage unity and joy. Common songs include “Paranauê” and “Zum Zum Zum.”
Classes
Capoeira for every age and experience level—toddlers, kids, adults, and private workshops.
Toddlers
Classes for toddlers focus on fun, movement, and music. Simple games and steps help children develop motor skills and confidence.
Kids & Teens
Kids and teens learn rhythm, kicks, acrobatics, and teamwork. Classes build discipline, musicality, and self-expression.
Adults
Classes for adults include advanced movement, music, and cultural learning. Students build fitness, resilience, and connection in a supportive community.
Private Lessons & Workshops
Private and small group sessions offer personalized learning for families, friends, or individuals. Workshops and Batizado events throughout the year.
