What is Capoeira?
Capoeira is an Afro-Brazilian art form that blends martial arts, dance, acrobatics, and music. Created by enslaved Africans in Brazil, Capoeira developed as a way to resist oppression and celebrate freedom. The game is played in a circle (roda), accompanied by music and singing, where two players exchange movements in dialogue, not combat. Capoeira is now celebrated worldwide for its creativity, community, and rich cultural heritage.
Music & Culture
Music is the heartbeat of Capoeira. The berimbau leads, with atabaque and pandeiro creating rhythm. Songs in Portuguese tell stories of struggle, joy, and unity. Students learn to sing, clap, and play instruments, connecting with the language and history. The roda is a space of respect, tradition, and celebration.
Movement & Fitness
Capoeira teaches fluid movement, agility, and strength. The foundational step is the "ginga", a rhythmic sway. Players learn kicks, escapes, acrobatics, and floor movements, always improvising and responding to each other's creativity. Classes develop core strength, flexibility, balance, and endurance—making Capoeira a full-body workout that's fun and accessible for all ages.
Benefits of Capoeira
  • Improves coordination, flexibility, and strength
  • Boosts confidence, discipline, and creativity
  • Encourages teamwork, friendship, and respect
  • Relieves stress through movement and music
  • Connects you to a vibrant, global community
  • Accessible and fun for kids, teens, and adults
Instrutor Arrepiado (Sagar Jain)

With over 14 years of Capoeira experience and 12 years of teaching, Sagar Jain (Instrutor Arrepiado) is passionate about sharing the art and energy of Capoeira across Pune and India. He is a direct student of Mestre Caxias (Grupo Capoeira Brasil), and regularly trains with world-renowned masters.

Sagar’s classes focus on discipline, rhythm, creativity, and joy. He welcomes students of all ages—from children finding confidence and coordination, to adults seeking a new challenge and community. He organizes workshops, performances, and Batizado events, and is dedicated to building a vibrant, inclusive Capoeira family in Pune.
