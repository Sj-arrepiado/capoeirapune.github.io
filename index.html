About Capoeira
A living art of movement, music, and culture
🌀
What is Capoeira?
Capoeira is a blend of martial arts, dance, acrobatics, and music—born in Brazil and celebrated worldwide.
Detailed Description:
Capoeira was created by enslaved Africans in Brazil as a way to disguise self-defense training with dance and music. Played in a circle called a roda, two players exchange movements in harmony with the music. It’s not just about fighting or dancing, but creativity, strategy, and expression. Today, Capoeira is practiced across the globe, recognized by UNESCO as cultural heritage. It develops agility, rhythm, and respect for tradition, bringing people together across ages and backgrounds.
🎶
Music & Culture
Learn to sing in Portuguese, play berimbau and drums, and experience the energy of the roda.
Detailed Description:
Music is the heartbeat of Capoeira. Traditional songs, rhythms, and instruments (such as berimbau, atabaque, and pandeiro) guide the game and set the mood. Students learn to sing, clap, and play instruments—connecting with the language and history of Capoeira. The culture teaches values of respect, resilience, and community.
🤸‍♂️
Movement & Fitness
Improve flexibility, strength, and coordination through playful movement and training.
Detailed Description:
Classes teach basic and advanced Capoeira movements—ginga, kicks, acrobatics, and escapes. Training develops core strength, stamina, balance, and creative expression. Movements are adapted for all ages and abilities, making Capoeira accessible and fun for everyone.
⭐
Benefits of Capoeira
Capoeira improves body and mind—fitness, confidence, focus, and friendship.
Detailed Description:
  • Builds physical fitness, flexibility, and motor skills
  • Boosts confidence, discipline, and creativity
  • Encourages teamwork, social skills, and respect
  • Relieves stress through movement and music
  • Connects you to a global community and culture
  • Fun for all ages—kids, teens, adults!
