What is Capoeira?
Capoeira is an Afro-Brazilian art form blending martial arts, dance, acrobatics, and music. Created by enslaved Africans in Brazil, Capoeira is about freedom, resilience, and creativity. Played in a circle (roda), two players exchange movements in a dialogue guided by music and song. More than a sport, Capoeira is a living tradition and a celebration of community.
Music & Culture
Music is at the heart of Capoeira. The berimbau sets the rhythm, atabaque and pandeiro add depth, and songs in Portuguese tell stories of struggle and joy. Every roda starts and ends with music, connecting players to Capoeira’s roots. In our classes, you’ll learn to play instruments, sing, and understand the meaning behind every song.
Movement & Fitness
Capoeira teaches fluid movement, agility, strength, and balance. The “ginga” is the basic step, a rhythmic sway that forms the foundation for kicks, escapes, and acrobatics. Classes are designed for all levels, focusing on core strength, flexibility, and creative expression. Movement is adapted for kids, teens, and adults alike.
Benefits of Capoeira
  • Improves coordination, flexibility, and strength
  • Boosts confidence, discipline, and creativity
  • Encourages teamwork, friendship, and respect
  • Relieves stress through movement and music
  • Connects you to a vibrant, global community
  • Accessible and fun for kids, teens, and adults
Instrutor Arrepiado (Sagar Jain)

With over 14 years of Capoeira experience and 12 years of teaching, Sagar Jain (Instrutor Arrepiado) is passionate about sharing the art and energy of Capoeira across Pune and India. He is a direct student of Mestre Caxias (Grupo Capoeira Brasil), and regularly trains with world-renowned masters.

Sagar’s classes focus on discipline, rhythm, creativity, and joy. He welcomes students of all ages—from children finding confidence and coordination, to adults seeking a new challenge and community. He organizes workshops, performances, and Batizado events, and is dedicated to building a vibrant, inclusive Capoeira family in Pune.
