What is Capoeira?

A martial art that looks like dancing — and feels like flying

Capoeira was born in Brazil, created by African slaves who hid their self-defense inside music and movement. Today it's one of the most unique art forms in the world — part fight, part dance, part game.

Two people enter a circle called the roda. Live music plays. And what happens next is a physical conversation — kicks and dodges, feints and flow — all in sync with the rhythm of the berimbau.

It's unlike anything you've tried before. And that's exactly why people fall in love with it.

Our Story

How Capoeira found its home in Pune

It started with a chance encounter. A visiting instructor — Monitor Atrasada — introduced Capoeira to Sagar right here in Pune. That first class changed everything.

Years of training under Mestre Caxias followed. And what began as one person's passion slowly became a community — kids, parents, young professionals, all drawn together by the same rhythm.

Today, Grupo Capoeira Brasil Pune is part of a global family spanning 40+ countries. But at its heart, it's still the same small circle of people who just love to move together.

Grupo Capoeira Brasil

A community in Pune,
connected to the world

Grupo Capoeira Brasil was founded in 1989 in Rio de Janeiro. Today it's one of the largest Capoeira organisations in the world — with groups in 40+ countries across six continents.

When you join us in Pune, you're not just joining a local class. You're stepping into a global network of capoeiristas who share the same values: respect, creativity, and community.

Every year, we host the Batizado — our annual grading ceremony — where students receive their cords and train alongside visiting mestres from around the world. It's one of the best days of the year, and everyone is welcome to be part of it.

Thinking about joining? Start now and you could be on the floor at the next Batizado — earning your first cord, surrounded by people cheering you on.
